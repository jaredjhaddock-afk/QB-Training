## Original game assets

Football-player geometry, equipment, uniforms, pose animations, turf textures,
stadium geometry, crowd arrangement, and field markings are authored in this
project. No paid assets, team trademarks, or Madden game assets are included.

All players use one shared geometry design and pose system. These are procedural
articulated players, not scanned athletes or motion-captured production models.
